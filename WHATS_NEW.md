# What's new in Road Safety Insights

**Version 0.3.0 · September 2026**

**Finds a new way when you take a different road**
Miss a turn or choose another road, and the app now works out a new route from where you are within a few seconds, instead of carrying on with directions for the road you left. The new route continues in the direction you're driving, so it won't tell you to turn around. While it's working, the screen says "Εκτός διαδρομής" (off route). Risky-spot warnings keep working the whole time.

---

## In version 0.2.0

**Version 0.2.0 · September 2026**

This update is all about driving with the app. Navigation is smoother, keeps going when you use other apps, can shrink into a small floating window, and warns you about risky spots even when the app isn't on screen. Finding the right place is easier too.

---

## Better navigation

**Smoother drive on the map**
The car arrow now glides along the road instead of jumping, and the map turns gently through corners. The blue route line starts right at your car and shrinks as you drive.

**Keeps going when you switch apps**
You can open music, messages or calls during a trip and navigation carries on. Pressing back no longer ends the trip. If your phone closes the app while you're away, the trip continues where it left off the next time you open it.

**Navigation in a small floating window**
Leave the app during a trip and the map shrinks into a small window you can move around the screen, like in Google Maps. It shows your car, the road ahead and your next turn, and turns orange when a risky spot is coming up. Tap it to go back to the full app.

**Warnings even in other apps**
When a risky spot is coming up and you're in another app, a notification pops up at the top of the screen. The navigation notification also shows your next turn and when you'll arrive. The first time you start a trip, the app asks to show notifications.

## Find places more precisely

**Better search**
Search now finds shops, hospitals and landmarks too. Each result shows the town underneath and how far away it is, and routes take you to the entrance.

**Pick an exact spot**
Press and hold anywhere on the map to go to exactly that spot. The app names it with the nearest address.

**Road numbers in directions**
Where a road has no name, directions now use its number, for example "ΕΟ3".

## Try it out: test drives

When you plan a route, you'll find three ready-made test drives that play in simulation and pass sample risky spots, so you can see the warnings in action:

- **Άρτα**: across the town centre to the General Hospital
- **Πύλη Βοιωτίας**: a short country drive to the Ζωοδόχου Πηγής monastery
- **Πύλη → Θήβα**: a longer drive of about 26 km

Try switching to another app during a test drive to see the floating window and the warning notifications.

## Fixed

**Warnings only on Naxos**
Warnings about risky spots during a trip only worked on Naxos. They now work everywhere there is sample data.

**Navigation lost after switching apps**
Leaving the app during a trip could send you back to the start screen. Your trip now carries on.

**Car in the wrong place on smaller screens**
With the phone sideways, in split screen or in a small window, the car could appear away from its spot on the route. It now stays in the right place at any size.

**Route line jumping around**
The blue route line could flicker or start ahead of or behind the car. It now always starts right at your car.

## Good to know

- **Simulation speed.** 1× now matches normal town driving (50 km/h). Choose 20× or 50× for the longer test drives.
- **House numbers.** In some towns the map data has no house numbers. If an address can't be found, press and hold on the map instead.
- **Sample information.** The accidents, road risk levels and rental cars shown are examples for testing, covering central Athens and Ioannina. The risky spots used for warnings cover Naxos, Άρτα, and the roads from Πύλη Βοιωτίας to Θήβα. None of these are real records yet.
- **Test version.** This version is for Android phones and is shared through Firebase App Tester. It is not in the Google Play Store yet.
- **Terms and Privacy Policy.** They still need the company's details added and a review by a lawyer before the public launch.

## In version 0.1.0

- **Roads coloured by risk**, from green (low) to red (very high), with a risk score out of 100 when you tap a road.
- **Accidents on the map** as dots coloured by how serious they were, grouped into numbered bubbles where there are many.
- **Filters** to show or hide accidents and risky roads, focus on serious accidents or recent years, or show only high-risk roads.
- **Car rentals from partners** in a panel at the bottom of the map, with special offers first.
- **New name, icon and welcome tour.** The app only asks for your location if you choose to share it.
- **Menu** with About, Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.
- **Compass and map rotation**, and easier tapping on coloured roads.
- **Fixes:** the app no longer closes by itself after using navigation, and the location button no longer jumps back to Athens.
